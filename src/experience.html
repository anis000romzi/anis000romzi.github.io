Backend Developer · PT. BSM Pialang Asuransi

January 2026 - Present

PT. BSM Pialang Asuransi is an insurance broker, and I work on the backend of the web apps that its staff and clients use to manage policies, file claims and refunds, and handle internal operations. My day-to-day is building and maintaining APIs mostly using PHP with the CodeIgniter framework, and I work closely with the business teams like insurance placement and claims to make sure the apps meet their needs.

The work I'm proudest of so far is on Portal Klaim, where the insured can track the progress and status of their insurance claims. The tricky part was getting the claims data across multiple databases and services, and making sure the data is accurate and up-to-date. I created endpoints for each service to look up claim status and progress, and then normalized the responses so the frontend could easily display them. The result is a single page where the insured can see all their claims and their status, and the business team can see the same data in their internal dashboard.

Full Stack Developer · PT. Mari Works Solution

September 2024 - January 2026

Mari Works is a software development company that builds web apps for clients and businesses. As a full stack developer there, I actually spent most of my time on the backend side of things.

My main project was TixFly, a software-as-a-service (SaaS) app for ticketing and event management. I worked mostly on the booking and payment side, which handled up to thousands of requests a day. The hard part was "ticket wars": when a popular event opens sales, lots of people try to buy at the same moment, and without safeguards two of them could both end up buying the last ticket. I used RabbitMQ to queue those purchases so they're processed in order, and also to let the app's services talk to each other. I added Redis caching to keep frequently read data fast.

None of this was rocket science, just well-known patterns, but it was my first time applying them to a real system with real traffic, and it taught me a lot about concurrency. I also worked on the admin dashboard, where event organizers can see reports on ticket sales, revenue, and attendance.

Full Stack Developer Intern · PT. Nusantara Infrastructure Tbk

August 2023 - December 2023

This was my first internship, during my studies at Bina Sarana Informatika (BSI), through MSIB Kampus Merdeka, a government internship program for university students. Nusantara Infrastructure works in infrastructure and energy fields like toll roads, renewable energy, and clean water. I joined the IT team to develop and maintain their internal apps.

My main project was NISA (Nusantara Infrastructure Self-Assessment), an internal web app for Nusantara Infrastructure staff to manage and monitor toll road operations. I worked on the repair and maintenance module, both the web app and the backend APIs for its mobile app. The app replaced a manual process that was done on paper. For example, for toll road repairs, the staff and field engineers used to fill out a paper form and manually ask the lead engineer to approve it. Now they can do it all in the app, and the lead engineer can approve it with a single click.

The app also has dashboards and reports for management to see the status of toll road operations, like CCTV monitoring, traffic data, and maintenance reports.

It was my first time working on a real codebase with a team, and it taught me a lot about how a professional software team works, like code reviews, version control, testing, and deployment.
