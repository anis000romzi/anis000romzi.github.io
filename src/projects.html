Portal Klaim

PT. BSM Pialang Asuransi · 2026

A web portal where the insured can track the status and progress of their insurance claims. The claims data is spread across several databases and services, so I built an endpoint for each one and normalized their responses into one format for the frontend. Now the insured and the business team see the same up-to-date claims in one place. Read more →

TixFly

PT. Mari Works Solution · 2024–2026

A SaaS platform for ticketing and event management. I worked on booking and payments, which handle up to thousands of requests a day. To stop two buyers from both getting the last ticket during "ticket wars", I queued purchases with RabbitMQ so they're processed in order, and added Redis caching for frequently read data. Read more →

Built using:
  • NestJS
  • Vue.js
  • PostgreSQL
  • Redis
  • RabbitMQ
Live site

NISA (Nusantara Infrastructure Self-Assessment)

PT. Nusantara Infrastructure Tbk · 2023

An internal web app for Nusantara Infrastructure staff to manage and monitor toll road operations. I built the repair and maintenance module, covering both the web app and the backend APIs for its mobile app. It replaced a paper process: repair requests that used to need a paper form and a manual sign-off are now approved in one click.
