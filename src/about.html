About Me

I studied Computer Science at Bina Sarana Informatika University, and these days I spend most of my time on the backend side of web development.

How I got into web development

It all started in my first year of university, when I watched freeCodeCamp's beginner Python tutorial on YouTube and something just clicked. The idea that I could build something from nothing but code was exciting, and I got hooked. Before long I was explaining programming to my classmates too, and I found out I really enjoy helping others. The more I explained things, the more I learned myself. I tried a bunch of languages and frameworks after that, but web development is what stuck, especially the backend. I like working on the part of an app that does the real work: the logic, the data, and how it all fits together.

My work style

When I write code, I try to keep it clean and easy for the next person to read and change, since that person is often future me. I'd rather take a little longer and get it right than rush it and spend twice as long fixing it later. I also like working with other people: bouncing ideas around, getting feedback, and explaining things.

What do I do outside of coding?

When I'm not coding, I'm probably playing an RPG. I love getting lost in another world and living a story through someone else's eyes. Fantasy is my favorite genre in pretty much any form, whether it's books, movies or games.
